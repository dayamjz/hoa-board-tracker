## Findings

**1. The irrigation proposal is not on the posted agenda.** Proposal 32084 is dated
October 28, one day before the meeting. Civil Code 4930 bars action on an item that
was not in the agenda distributed with the meeting notice.

**2. The hearing notice for 24150 Amaranth Loop carries two defects.** It sets a
written-response deadline of 1/1/2025, a date already past when the notice went out
October 2, and cites Civil Code 1368, repealed effective 2014 and renumbered to 4525.
Ten-day notice under Civil Code 5855 was met.

**3. The mulch draw exceeds its reserve component by $15,057.60.** Component 3405.02
holds $2,008.80. The fund as a whole has the cash; the component plan does not.

**4. Landscape supplies and repairs is the durable overrun.** Account 6020 has spent
$21,994.25 against a $12,000 annual budget, $9,994.25 past the full year with four
months left. The irrigation proposal would take it to roughly $29,570.

**5. August's loss is mostly timing.** CCTV billed its full annual $9,600 in August
against an $800 monthly budget and is exactly on budget for the year. Water billed
$9,397.88 after a $0 July and remains $6,428.26 under budget. Tree maintenance at
$5,324.50 in one month is still $2,416.39 under budget year to date.

**6. The 2027 budget balances to exactly zero, and the mix is optimistic.** Income and
expense both sit at $291,000, up $5,820 on a $5 per unit monthly increase across 97
units. Landscape is budgeted at $72,100 while 2026 annualizes to roughly $87,800, a gap
of about $15,700 absorbed by slack elsewhere. There is no cushion beyond the $3,500
contingency line.

**7. The 2027 budget must reach members by December 1.** Civil Code 5300 requires
distribution 30 to 90 days before fiscal year end. Approving October 29 leaves 33 days.

**8. No reserve study work has been billed in 2026.** Account 7055 shows $0 against a
$750 budget, and the 2027 draft repeats the $750. Civil Code 5550 requires a visual
site inspection at least every three years and an annual review.

**9. Four reserve components are already negative.** Light bollards -$16,335.74,
contingency reserve -$41,962.37, street lights -$3,325.86, irrigation controllers
-$650.37.

**10. The 2027 budget has no line for social events.** $1,500 was approved in August at
$500 per event. The nearest fit is contingency at $3,500.

**11. Minor corrections.** The August minutes record the lien account as 1168006801;
the lien packet shows 116800680. The Member at Large seat is vacant. No annual
membership meeting or election date appears in the 2027 schedule, though electronic
voting was adopted in August.
